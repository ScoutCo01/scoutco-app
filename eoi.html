How it works

  1. Tell us about youFill in the form below. It takes about 10 minutes and covers your goals, training, equipment and health.
  2. Have a chatWe read your answers and get in touch to talk through your goals, answer your questions and make sure online coaching is the right fit.
  3. Get set upYou get your own link to the ScoutCo app. Save it to your home screen and sign your agreement right there in the app.
  4. TrainYour program shows up in the app week by week. Tick off your sets, log your weights and see what you lifted last time.
  5. Check in every weekFrom Friday, a quick check-in on your sleep, nutrition, energy, soreness and head noise. Your coach replies personally and adjusts your program from what you tell them.
  6. Review and planA one-on-one call with your coach each 4-week phase to look at how it's going and plan what's next.

Good to know

Do I need a gym?

No. We write your program around what you have, whether that's a full gym, a home setup or a few dumbbells. Tell us what you've got in the form.

How long is a block?

Training runs in 12-week blocks and renews automatically. Give 2 weeks' notice if you're not renewing.

How do I pay?

Pay upfront, or weekly or fortnightly in instalments.

Would I be better off face to face?

You can also mix online coaching with a face-to-face session with us every week or fortnight. Mention it in the form and we'll talk you through the options.

Does filling this in sign me up?

No. This is just to register your interest. Nothing is booked or paid until you've had a chat with us and decided to go ahead.
